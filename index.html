🚀 AI Video Creator Masterclass & Viral Blueprint

Learn the exact Hollywood and YouTube multi-clip workflow used to turn 5-second AI video clips into 1-minute viral Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and cinematic videos with voiceovers.

🎬

1. 5s Clips Se 1-Min Video Kaise Banayein?

AI models 5-second scenes render karte hain. Long video banane ka professional 3-step formula:

  • 1 Shot Planning: Apni story ko 6 scenes mein banto (6 scenes × 5s = 30 seconds).
  • 2 Angle Variation: Har clip mein camera angle change karo (Drone shot ➔ Close-up ➔ Action shot).
  • 3 CapCut / Premiere Merge: Sabhi 5s clips ko CapCut mein line se laga kar smooth cross-dissolve transitions add karo.
🎙️

2. Free AI Voiceover & Sound Design

Top free tools jahan se realistic voice aur background music lagana hai:

  • 1 ElevenLabs (Free): Best realistic AI human voices (English & Hindi/Urdu).
  • 2 CapCut Text-to-Speech: App ke andar direct text likho aur viral narrator voice select karo.
  • 3 Freesound.org / Pixabay: Free cinematic sound effects (whoosh, rain, car engine, bass drops).
💎

3. Hollywood Prompt Engineering Formula

Har prompt mein ye 4 elements zaroor dalo taaki video high-quality aaye:

[Subject] + [Action/Motion] + [Lighting & Environment] + [Camera & 8k Quality]

4. Instagram & YouTube Growth Secret

AI content ko million views tak pahunchane ke proven algorithm tips:

  • 1 First 3-Second Hook: Pehle scene mein fast action ya bold question dalo taaki scroll stop ho.
  • 2 Aspect Ratio Rule: Reels & Shorts ke liye 768x1280 (9:16) ya 768x768 (1:1) use karo.
  • 3 Best Posting Times: 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM aur 6:00 PM – 8:30 PM par post karo.
